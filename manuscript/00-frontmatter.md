## A note on the numbers

Three rules governed every page of this book.

First, every number comes from a citable public source: a peer-reviewed paper, a government statistical agency, or a named research institution. Every one carries an endnote telling you exactly where it lives, so you can check it yourself.

Second, two ideas are never allowed to blur into each other. *Exposure* is what AI could do to the tasks in a job, on paper. *Displacement* is what is actually happening to employment, in the records. Most of the fear and most of the hype in this debate comes from confusing the two.

Third, when the evidence is contested, you get the contest, not just the side that makes the better headline.

The charts in this book are rebuilt from downloadable data; the sources and scripts are listed in the endnotes. Figures are current as of the data vintages named in the text, generally mid-to-late 2026. Labor data moves. The method for reading it doesn't.
